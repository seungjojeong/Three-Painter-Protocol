Rights and permissions

Three Painter Protocol · Jesse Ash, Rebecca Fortnum, Seungjo Jeong

Copyright

Copyright in each painting remains with the artist who made it. Jesse Ash, Rebecca Fortnum and Seungjo Jeong each retain all rights in their own works.

Copyright in all photographs on this site — both the installation views and the images of individual works — is held by Seungjo Jeong.

Reproducing an image therefore involves two rights: the artist’s, in the painting, and the photographer’s, in the photograph. Both are reserved.

Permitted use

Installation views and images of works may be reproduced for editorial, press and non-commercial academic purposes, provided that:

  • the artist, title, year and medium are given as shown in the caption;
  • the photographer is credited;
  • the exhibition, Three Painter Protocol at The Transformation Gallery, is named; and
  • the image is reproduced in full, without cropping, overprinting or alteration.

All other use

Commercial reproduction, use in training data for machine learning systems, and any use not covered above require written permission in advance from the artist and the photographer.
