Three Painter Protocol

Jesse Ash, Rebecca Fortnum, Seungjo Jeong

The protocol

Three painters, Jesse Ash, Rebecca Fortnum, Seungjo Jeong, circulated paintings between their studios. Taking another’s existing painting as a reference, each painter made a response; the reference was then passed on to the third painter, who responded in turn.

Through phases 1 and 2 each response was made in the presence of the physical painting rather than from a photographic reproduction: the painting travelled to the responding painter’s studio and remained there for the duration of the phase.

Before the final phase the three references and the six responses were brought together at one studio, so the painters could see them side by side and share each other’s work in progress.

Phase 3 turned the process back on itself. Jesse and Rebecca responded to the exchange as a whole; Seungjo responded to a painting made in response to his own. Twelve paintings result: three reference works, six responses and three made in the final phase.

The exhibition

The Transformation Gallery, Arch 18 Valentia Place, London SW9 8PJ

8–25 October 2026 · Thursday to Sunday, 14:00–18:00

All twelve paintings were shown together: the three that began the project and the nine it produced.

The accompanying text, “Opening to the Unexpected: Nine Notes on a Protocol-Based Practice”, was written by Emma Cocker, writer-artist and Associate Professor in Fine Art at Nottingham Trent University. It is published on the gallery’s page for the exhibition.

The opening reception was held on Thursday 8 October 2026, 18:00–21:00.
