Three Painter Protocol

Jesse Ash, Rebecca Fortnum, Seungjo Jeong

The protocol

Three painters, Jesse Ash, Rebecca Fortnum, Seungjo Jeong, circulated physical paintings between their studios. Taking another’s existing painting as a reference, each painter made a response; the reference was then passed on to the third painter, who responded in turn.

Through phases 1 and 2 each response was made in the presence of the physical painting rather than from a photographic reproduction: the painting travelled to the responding painter’s studio and remained there for the duration of the phase.

Phase 3 turned the process back on itself. Jesse and Rebecca responded to the exchange as a whole; Seungjo responded to a painting made in response to his own. Twelve paintings result: three reference works, six responses and three made in the final phase.
